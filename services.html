Our Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Services along with their benefits:

VISUAL TESTING (VT)

Benefits :

➢ Simple and cost-effective

➢ Requires minimal equipment

➢ Immediate defect detection

ULTRASONIC TESTING (UT)

Benefits :

➢ Detects internal and surface defects

➢ High penetration depth

➢ Suitable for a variety of materials

MAGNETIC PARTICLE TESTING (MT)

Benefits :

➢ Quick and reliable for surface and near-surface defects

➢ High sensitivity for ferromagnetic materials

➢ Portable and easy to use

PENETRANT TESTING (PT)

Benefits :

➢ Cost-effective and simple

➢ Detects fine cracks and surface defects

➢ Applicable to various non-porous materials

THERMOGRAPHIC TESTING (IR INFRARED TESTING)

Benefits :

➢ Detects temperature variations indicating hidden defects

➢ Non-contact and fast inspection

➢ Useful for electrical and mechanical systems

. PROOF LOADING OF EQUIPMENT & CERTIFICATION

Technique :

➢ ses solid weights (e.g., steel blocks, concrete, or calibrated test weights) to apply a known load.

➢ The equipment is loaded beyond its rated capacity (typically 1.25 to 1.5 times) for a specified duration.

Benefits :

➢ Simple and highly accurate

➢ Provides direct and reliable verification of load capacity
